Methodology and source

The located workbook describes CTI as the arithmetic mean of ten equally weighted pillars measuring creative work characteristics. It states that national survey-weighted pillar shares are indexed against the simple average of national shares across EU27, with a reference of 100. This is not a worker-population-weighted EU aggregate.

This edition preserves the overall CTI scores extracted from the workbook. It does not reconstruct microdata, validate survey coding, estimate uncertainty, or imply that country differences are statistically significant. Creative work characteristics are not the same as employment exclusively in cultural and creative industries.
